Revenue = customers × ARPU; Opex split 60% customer-variable / 40% fixed, inflating annually; cost synergies are a one-off percentage reduction of the pre-synergy cost base, phased in 25/50/75/100% across 2027–30, and therefore do not offset compounding inflation year on year; UFCF = NOPAT + D&A − Capex − ΔNWC at 30% tax, D&A 3.0%, Capex 3.5% and NWC 5.0% of revenue. 2030 exit EV = UFCF 2031 / (WACC − g). Entry debt = leverage × EBITDA 2026, amortised on a 100% cash sweep; 100% acquisition, no vendor loan and no management rollover. Maximum entry equity = 2030 exit equity / target MOIC.
